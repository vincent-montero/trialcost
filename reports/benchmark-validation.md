## Notes per check

- **aspe_cns_ph2_pp** (48,767 USD/patient (2018 USD)): Weighted mean over CNS phase 2 trials (mixture of designs); reference design matches N = 243 and duration, procedures and countries are assumptions
- **aspe_all_ph2_pp** (58,618 USD/patient (2018 USD)): All therapeutic areas (N = 235, 34 months); CNS reference design used as proxy
- **aspe_cns_ph3_pp** (39,612 USD/patient (2018 USD)): Weighted mean over CNS phase 3 trials; reference design matches N = 529 and duration
- **moore_pivotal_median** (19,000,000 USD/trial (2016 USD)): Median of 138 pivotal trials (median N = 488, all areas, a mix of uncontrolled, placebo- and active-controlled trials; proportions not reported); price year not stated, trials 2015-16 assumed
- **moore_pivotal_pp** (41,117 USD/patient (2016 USD)): Median per patient over 138 pivotal trials
- **moore_pivotal_ppv** (3,562 USD/patient-visit (2016 USD)): Median per patient-visit; model counts expected visits incl. screening and remote
- **martin_ph2_median** (8,600,000 USD/trial (2015 USD)): Median phase II trial cost (2010-15 trials, all areas); median trial size not available (paywalled), price year assumed 2015
- **tufts_ph2_day** (23,737 USD/day (2024 USD)): Tufts phase 2 'direct cost per day' (via Clinical Leader): definition of the day base unknown; model = total / (T_total in days)
- **moore_small_mean** (5,900,000 USD/trial (2016 USD)): Mean of pivotal trials with <= 100 patients (archetype N = 50, placebo-controlled, MRI); mixture includes uncontrolled trials
- **moore_min_orphan** (5,000,000 USD/trial (2016 USD)): Uncontrolled orphan trials with < 15 patients: much smaller than the archetype, shown for scale only
- **aspe_cns_ph2_duration** (40.1 months (no price year)): ASPE phase duration vs model study start to CSR
- **aspe_all_ph2_duration** (34 months (no price year)): All-areas phase 2 duration; CNS reference design used as proxy
- **aspe_cns_ph3_duration** (45.3 months (no price year)): ASPE phase duration vs model study start to CSR

## Caveats

- A benchmark is a mean or median over many real trials with different designs; the model band is parameter uncertainty for one design. Agreement within ~±30 % is the most that can be expected; a systematic gap in one direction is the useful signal.
- Reference designs (`designs/ref-*.yaml`) are assumptions (README §6 #19).
- Benchmarks are US-centric; reference designs mix US and EU sites.
